Supplier & Procurement Projects

Two practical projects showing how supplier and inventory data can be structured into decision-support tools.

01 · Inventory · Supplier Performance

Material Planning & Supplier Performance Analytics

Business problem: identify shortages, delivery delays and stock-coverage risks before they become operational problems.

  • Tracks forecast demand against confirmed supplier supply.
  • Flags late and short deliveries using formula-driven status logic.
  • Summarises stock coverage by SKU.
  • Provides a supplier-delay what-if scenario for planning.

Tools & methods: Excel · SUMIFS · COUNTIFS · IF logic · conditional formatting · data validation

02 · Procurement · Sustainability

Supplier Sustainability Scorecard

Business problem: compare suppliers using structured sustainability and delivery-performance criteria.

  • Combines environmental and operational supplier indicators.
  • Uses weighted scoring to create a composite supplier score.
  • Includes on-time delivery alongside sustainability measures.
  • Turns procurement requirements into measurable KPIs.

Tools & methods: Excel · weighted scoring · KPI design · conditional formatting
