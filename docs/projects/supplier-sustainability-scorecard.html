Project 02 · Procurement · Sustainability

Supplier Sustainability Scorecard

This Excel project explores a structured way to compare suppliers using sustainability indicators together with operational delivery performance.

Business problem

How can procurement teams translate sustainability requirements into measurable supplier KPIs?

Decision support

A weighted composite score that combines environmental and delivery-performance criteria.

What the workbook contains

  • Supplier-level sustainability indicators
  • Weighted scoring model
  • Operational delivery-performance input
  • Composite supplier assessment
  • Conditional formatting for comparison

Techniques

Weighted scoring, KPI design, normalization and conditional formatting.
